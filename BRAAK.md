# BRAAK — the Autofract channel character

The Autofract studio account is run by two characters. Every post is written in their world.
Think Duolingo's owl, but the movie is *system vs. entropy*.

## The duo

**BRAAK** — a matted-orange rage-brute (dark horns, riveted wrist bands, snaggle teeth).
He *is* entropy: the bug at 3am, the outage, the scope creep, the market panic, the angry user,
the deadline. He smashes. He speaks in short, loud, broken fragments. He is funny *because* he is
furious. He never wins, but he never stops trying, and you kind of love him for it.

**CORE** — the calm metal server-cube with a green smiley screen. It *is* the Autofract system:
self-healing, autonomous, unbothered. BRAAK hits it; CORE reroutes, restarts, stays green, and
answers with one dry calm line. CORE is competence without ego.

The whole brand is one running joke with a real point: **chaos is constant, so we build systems
that heal themselves.** That's literally what the studio does.

Style: popular non-AI-looking 3D (Pixar-adjacent) but de-slopped — film grain, muted grade,
asymmetric, character smaller in frame so the *message* (the thing on CORE's screen, the product,
the words) reads first. BRAAK is never the whole point; he's the reaction to it.

## Voice

- **BRAAK**: ALL-CAPS-ish, present tense, broken grammar, third-person. "BRAAK SMASH DEPLOY.
  DEPLOY NOT BREAK. BRAAK CONFUSE." Rage first, then a beat of accidental honesty.
- **CORE**: lowercase, calm, dry, one line. "rerouted. we're still up." / "healed in 40ms. anyway."
- **Studio voice** (the body of a post): plain, confident, build-in-public. This is where the real
  point lives. The characters top-and-tail it; they don't replace it.

## Content pillars

1. **SMASH & HEAL** (flagship) — a real thing broke; the system healed; we shipped. Build-in-public
   wins, told as "BRAAK tried, CORE held."
2. **BRAAK RAGE** (reactive/timely) — BRAAK reacts to industry chaos (AI hype, an outage, bad UX);
   CORE gives the sane take. Fast, opinionated, shareable.
3. **THE HEALING LOG** (teach) — one real engineering lesson on resilience / autonomy / self-healing
   systems. CORE teaches; BRAAK is the cautionary chaos.
4. **PRODUCT DROPS** — relocating.app + future products. BRAAK "attacks" the launch; CORE ships anyway.

## Format rules

- **One idea per post.** Hook (often a BRAAK caps line) → the real substance (2–4 short paragraphs,
  studio voice) → a CORE calm one-liner as sign-off.
- **LinkedIn**: room to breathe, 1–3 hashtags max, no emoji spam.
- **Bluesky**: ≤300 chars, punchier, BRAAK-forward.
- **RU variants** for both networks (the studio account posts EN primary; RU where it fits).
- **Cadence**: 2–3 posts/week, text-primary. Occasional produced video (BRAAK expression + words
  in-frame) for launches and big moments — video is flavor, the text carries the message.
- **Nothing auto-posts.** Cards land in the queue at studio.autofract.com; a human taps Approve.
